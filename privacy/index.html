Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-30.

Maple Porch Puzzles does not ask for your name, email address or any account. The word search and sudoku makers run in your own browser; the words you type are not sent to us or stored anywhere.

Fonts

We load the Lexend font from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download it. See the Google privacy policy.

Advertising

This site does not currently show ads. If that changes, this page will explain how ads use cookies before any ads appear.

Links to Amazon

Some pages link to our printed puzzle books on Amazon. Amazon may set cookies when you follow those links.

Hosting

Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running.
