Why large print?

Most puzzle books squeeze the letters to fit more on a page. We do the opposite: much bigger letters, plenty of space between them, and word lists you can read without hunting for your glasses. Our puzzles are made for grandparents, for anyone with tired eyes, and for activity sessions in libraries and care homes.

Every puzzle is built by our own software and checked so that each word appears exactly once, so the answer key is never ambiguous.
