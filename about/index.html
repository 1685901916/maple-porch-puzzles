About Maple Porch Puzzles

Maple Porch Puzzles makes puzzles that are easy on the eyes. We started with a simple observation: many puzzle lovers stop doing word searches and sudoku not because they lose interest, but because the print gets too small.

Everything on this site is free to print for personal use, for classrooms, and for activity programs in libraries, senior centers and care homes. You don't need an account, and the puzzle makers run entirely in your browser.

How our puzzles are made

Our puzzles are generated by software we wrote ourselves. Word searches are checked so each word appears exactly once, and easy word searches never hide words backwards. Every sudoku has exactly one solution; easy and medium ones can be solved step by step without guessing.
