About the levels

Easy and Medium puzzles can always be finished step by step without guessing: at every point there is a square with only one possible number. Medium simply gives you fewer numbers to start with. Hard puzzles have fewer clues and may need a pencil and some patience. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.
